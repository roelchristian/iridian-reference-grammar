# Articleless reference: migration record

Base revision: `2ea86f3046beff0d55ee9f7f7e0f06bb31932e26` (merged PRs 24–25).
This reform changes only definiteness, grammatical anchoring and their direct
nominal, semantic and discourse dependencies. The base's y/ė inventory,
é–í and ó–ú contrasts, uo, verbal quantities and case alignment remain in force.

## Modern nominal inventory

An ordinary nominal has its established case–number form. Definite, indefinite,
speaker-specific, anaphoric and generic/kind interpretations follow from the
construction and context; no article, silent article, replacement suffix or
specificity proclitic is supplied. DP remains a cover term, rather than proof
of a universal D head. Demonstratives identify referents without definiteness
agreement; possessors retain their independent indirect case and interpretation.
Topics, grammatical pivots and contextual identification remain distinct.

## Deleted morphology and restored forms

The productive shared/speaker/unmarked anchoring feature opposition, dedicated
Anch projection, speaker-anchoring si, and its nominal-domain linearisation
rules are removed. Only the anchoring functions of -o/-e/-to/-te and cumulative
-ó/-é/-á disappear. Homophonous lexical vowels, case endings and derivation
remain. No string-wide replacement of suffixes was used.

The [baseline and complete cell mapping](articleless-review/morphology-baseline.json)
records all 102 ordinary cells and their former counterparts in five shared
tables. Every retained ordinary cell is compared exactly with that baseline.
Representative repairs include:

| Former anchored use | Ordinary replacement | Distinction preserved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bylo | byl | Direct singular child; ordinary plural byle remains |
| bylato | byla | Transitive singular |
| travó | trava | Direct singular; progressive patient travě remains indirect |
| Marko / Markuto | Marėk / Marku | Current citation stem and transitive proper-name irregularity |
| ránete | ráne | Ordinary Class V indirect, not deletion of a final vowel |
| pivó | pivo | Class VIII direct singular |
| lúbko / pérko | lobėk / perėk | Independently stored Class II direct citation stems |
| tězno / oblo | tězėň / obėl | Class VII citation stems, where those anchored forms occurred |
| pjáščikovat / pjaštovat | pjáščikou / pjaštou | Ordinary participant/event direct singular |
| -ovyte / -ověte nominal alternatives | -ovy / -ově | Already established deverbal obliques only |

The ordinary singular deverbal cells remain; continuous participant pjaštova is
still specified only in direct singular, and neither missing obliques nor
plurals were inferred from abolition of anchoring. The unrelated relative
exponent -ovy and its selection restrictions remain protected.

## Examples, interpretation and structure

Chapter 4 retains the nine declensions, singular/plural, three cases, vocative
residues and proper-name irregularities. Chapter 5 replaces Anch lexical entries,
trees and si domains with articleless composition. The same Byl zdrávime is used
in newly introduced and previously identified child contexts. Chapters 7 and 14
retain perfective Trava Marku pjaštník versus progressive Marėk travě pjaštíme;
their case difference continues to follow argument structure, not definiteness.
Chapter 17 connects introduction, bare resumption, null continuation,
competition and overt reactivation without asserting a new categorical
anaphoric hierarchy. Existential pivots, nominal predication, negation and
clause-linkage examples are reconciled, including literal utility inputs.

Removed labels naming obsolete morphology are repaired at their incoming
references; generic reference labels are retained where their sections still
serve the original semantic topic. Appendix A now defines the retained nominal
layers without an obligatory Anch or silent D, and current DEF/Sanch glosses and
lexicon entries are retired. Schematic si/ši in the phonology remains a sound
contrast, not a surviving determiner.

## Historical boundary

unadopted nominalisation proposal, the implicit-possession research review,
the grammar metahistory, central-vowel migration and related pivot/clause-linking
records retain their
original bodies beneath explicit status preambles. Original-body hashes guard
these boundaries. Historical comparison is not normative morphology, while the
historical appendix's tentative stem-levelling account is not a new chronology
or an established sound law.

## Analytical limits

A design decision about Iridian is not an attested judgement of Hindi,
Kurmanji, Lithuanian or another articleless language. Comparative discussions
remain source-specific. In particular, lack of articles does not prove lack of
D; speaker specificity does not automatically establish wide scope; bridging,
institutional readings and covariation still need their stated independent
diagnostics. No differential object marking or new agreement was introduced.
