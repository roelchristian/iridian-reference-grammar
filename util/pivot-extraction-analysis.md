long-distance extraction, extraction from islands, or universal cleft and
coordination accessibility.

## Matching features and locality

A comparative implementation must give Top a discourse/aboutness dependency
and the wh dependency an interrogative one. Direct case alone does not make a
nominal a matching goal of either dependency. Thus a selected direct patient
need not block a linked agent merely because it is pronounced first. This is a
conditional intervention analysis, not an independently established inventory
of formal features. If a patient also bears the relevant discourse or
interrogative feature, the analysis must state its competition and stopping
conditions rather than silently ignoring it.

Skipping a nonmatching DP is distinct from accessing material inside a
transferred phase. No feature contrast by itself supplies a phase edge. Any
finite derivation crossing an embedded domain must specify accessible edges,
independently justified transparency, or a different locality architecture.
The allowed local agent topicalisation cannot license arbitrary extraction
through a causative or a finite complement.

## The separate pre-by problem

The inventory `PolP > FocP > IP`, with head-initial Pol, predicts `by` before
material solely in its FocP complement. It does not derive either overt-by
string above merely by choosing in-situ versus SpecFocP wh placement. Moving
only a wh-expression above Pol also fails to derive the order `Marek jena by
mulša` while preserving Marek ahead of it without further independently
motivated structure or displacement.

Three possibilities remain open, with explicit costs:

1. A higher interrogative/discourse position: needs independent evidence for
   its distinction from TopP and lower FocP, and must derive both overt-by
   orders with the correct interpretation.
2. Construction-sensitive placement of the overt particle: needs a stated PF
   linearisation condition which preserves interrogative scope and the polar
   question distribution. It must not retroactively change narrow syntax.
3. A revision of the earlier structural identification of the pre-by phrase:
   needs a complete derivation for the four orders, while keeping optional
   fronting separate from interrogative licensing.

No possibility is adopted as canonical in this revision. Arbitrary additional
movements introduced only to reproduce a string would not decide between them.
The surface paradigm has greater evidential status than the incomplete tree.
The main grammar records the limit compactly; these alternatives remain here.
