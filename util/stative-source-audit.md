# Stative reform: primary-source audit

Checked 29 October 2026. The Iridian paradigms are constructed grammatical
specifications. None of the comparative sources attests an Iridian exponent,
quantity alternation, consonant repair, case frame or construction. Comparative
analyses motivate distinctions to test; they do not establish the proposed
language's rules. Access levels below distinguish article text from an indexed
abstract and bibliographical metadata.

## Adoption boundary

The strongest comparative support is for distinguishing lexical states,
overt transitions, scalar degree change, and causation, and for making
experiential perspective sensitive to context. A reduced-property/full-aspectual
relative contrast, result-state entailments and Iridian allomorphy remain
language-internal specifications. No source establishes a historical liquid
suffix class, a third attributive grade, irregular stative classes, or a new
selection frame for analytic *neh-*; those features require independent evidence
and remain outside the normative implementation.
