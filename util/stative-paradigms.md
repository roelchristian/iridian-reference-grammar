# Stative paradigm and selection audit

These are constructed grammatical specifications, not native attestations. The independent literal matrix is `scripts/stative-fixtures.json`; `scripts/stative_diagnostics.py` checks selected phonological tokens and supplied semantic conditions. It is a bounded reference model, not a general analyser or a pronunciation generator.

## Adopted selection

- The seven short stems below have explicitly adopted finite Class A principal parts. Their licensed simple-state inventory is continuous, retrospective and contemplative, retaining G0 with invariant retrospective `-ais` and long contemplative `-áž`. Finite class does not determine attributive class.
- Protected `tvírzn-` has lexical `í`, short continuous and contemplative endings, and invariant retrospective `-ais`. Its separately selected simple attributive `tvírzná` retains both long vowels.
- Become takes base-grade State, selecting `-él-` after an unprotected short stem and `-el-` with independently protected length. Its short outer endings are `-a/-ime/-ek/-až`, alongside invariant retrospective `-ais`; Aspect does not reach through Become to regrade the property root.
- Free inchoative PF `-ek` has ordinary bound `-ik-` before participant `-ou` and relative `-ovy`, and bound `-ice` before the direct linker. Simple-state perfective is unavailable; eventive derivatives retain their own perfective allomorphy.
- Negative indicative `za-` scopes above Asp, remains unstressed, and precedes every consonant-initial stem here. `vy` and `so` are external phasal particles. Neither supplies a covert morphological Become, although phase over a state can convey an onset as a discourse inference.
- Simple-state progressive and perfective are unavailable, including in a temporary, expressive or bounded context; no such context supplies a missing event predicate. A retrospective state does not entail entry into the state; contemplative is projective rather than a future tense.
- A continuous State relative is marked and needs explicit episodic, temporal or contrastive context. A simple property attributive has a smaller projection. Full relative means Asp-containing within the existing reduced selection boundary: internal Neg, Mood, Evid, Phas and comment operators remain excluded.
- Participant `-ou` abstracts the independently selected participant (the sole holder in underived State/Become); it does not automatically denote a state, transition event or result. Lexical/event `-ou` formations require their own construction and interpretation. Res requires a preceding event where lexical openness need not.
- Ordinary statives leave information source unspecified. The indirect evidential is independently available, without a grammatical person restriction or a prescribed source preference for psychological predicates. Experiencer, information source and outward manifestation remain distinct.

## PF and boundary audit

`muž + n` preserves `žn`. The independently existing obstruent–nasal onset licences `mu.žna`; there is no deletion of the stativiser, exceptional epenthesis or automatic ž mutation. Short `u` is underlying /ʊ/, strong `ú` /uː/. Illustrative careful-speech forms are `[ˈmʊ.ʒna]`, `[ˈmuː.ʒna]`, `[ˈmʊ.ʒneː.la]` and `[ˈmʊ.ʒneː.lɪ.tsɛ]` for state, simple attribute, inchoative continuous and linked PF respectively. Quantity does not attract stress to the Become vowel.

`za-` leaves lexical initial stress on the following stem; an integrated degree prefix takes the initial stress of the expanded lexical word. Thus `zamužna` retains stress on `mu-`, while `zapremužna` retains stress on `pre-`. The tests use underlying selected segment tokens; syllabification and stress judgments in this paragraph are explicit manual applications of the existing phonology, not output from the token helper.

Plain Become attachment preserves the final nasal. Manner `-ě` independently selects n→ň: `mužně`. Direct PF linking first selects bound `-ik-`, then front-grade k→c once: `mužnélice`, excluding `*mužnéliče`. With Evid intervening, `mužnélekal` instead gives `mužnélekalě`: PAL targets the new final l and cannot select inner `-ik-`. Progressive `mužnélime` gives direct `mužnélimně`, but evidential `mužnélimelě` retains the full inner ending. The same explicit distinction is included for every finite cell in the literal matrix.
