## Corrections to the supplied plan

- **Coon, Baier and Levin is 2021, not 2026.** The [Cambridge article page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/language/article/abs/mayan-agent-focus-and-the-ergative-extraction-constraint-facts-and-fictions-revisited/7EBA1153EB80EDEB4C4A14DE08F58163) now says first published online 1 January 2026, but gives the original issue as *Language* 97(2), June 2021, 269–332, copyright 2021, DOI [10.1353/lan.2021.0019](https://doi.org/10.1353/lan.2021.0019). The later platform date must not be mistaken for new research. Its abstract proposes object intervention involving a bundled Ā/D probe in a subset of Mayan languages; Iridian need not have that probe.
- **Miyagawa's date depends on the bibliographic convention.** [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262513555/why-agree-why-move/) gives 30 October 2009; the author's [monograph page](https://www.shigerumiyagawa.com/new-page) calls it Miyagawa (2010), as do copyright-based catalogues. Use 2009 consistently with the publisher release metadata and note the alternate 2010 citation; do not describe this as a newly discovered contradiction in the theory.
- **Clem and Deal has several circulation dates.** The [publisher's 2026 issue](https://direct.mit.edu/ling/issue/57/2) places the article in 57(2):267–313. Earlier records refer to an advance article in 2024/2025. The [author's paper list](https://pages.ucsd.edu/~eclem/papers.html) still says “to appear”. Cite the final 2026 issue consistently. The long DOI supplied by the brief is associated with that work; the 2026 agreement review also uses a shortened `ling0529` DOI route. Do not count these as separate works.

## Argument introduction, existential comparison and DM: supplementary check
