# Clause-linking reform: editorial change record

## Phase I — Inventory audit

The canonical morphology table in Chapter 16 contained sixteen distinct endings: `-u`, `-ení`, `-ěc`, `-ezak`, `-eškady`, `-eeby`, `-ešhoume`, `-eebymy`, `-esny`, `-ouc`, `-ic`, `-emy`, `-ezmy`, `-ena`, and `-it`, together with contrastive `-ema`. Its four-member conditional row contradicted the two-member paradigm developed later in the same chapter.

The productive core now contains exactly `-u`, `-ení`, `-ěc`, `-ic`, `-ezmy`, `-it`, `-ouc`, and `-esny`. The unsupported conditional entries `-emy` and `-ena` have been withdrawn; contrast is expressed by existing adversative coordination or concession instead of `-ema`. Searches of current chapters found no examples or incoming references requiring these three endings. The existing contrast-section label is preserved. State-frame `-eň`, finite `-e`, and discourse `nu` are explicitly outside the productive paradigm.

The five specialised temporal endings remain separately documented during this intermediate phase. Their removal depends on Phase IV coverage of temporal event nominals, especially endpoint `do`; exclusion from the productive core does not yet constitute complete withdrawal from the description. No diachronic or register status is inferred for withdrawn design elements. The erroneous claim that temporal suffixes all contain finite linker `-e` has been removed without replacing it with an unverified segmentation.

## Decisions and remaining limits

- Circumstantial relevance is conventional in `-ení`; result persistence and event causation are contextual inferences.
- Temporal `z` defaults to completion and `do` to onset, with an explicitly identified alternative boundary permitted. Event nominals preserve nominal case and have no independent finite tense or clausal negation domain.
- Expected `-ezmy` does not assert antecedent occurrence; prospective `-esny` does not entail actualisation of its reference event.
- Finite `-e` retains the null-C dependency analysis and local finite inflection. Autonomous assertion, outer evidential perspective, and independently scoped directive/interrogative force remain unspecified.
- Propositional anaphora and its proposed connective family lack a licensed lexical exponent; no automatic extension of nominal `i/ji/ni` is adopted.
- Independent internal negation and concord in converbs remain unspecified. The diagnostics do not add an unverified paradigm.
- No dedicated causal suffix, new phonological alternation, historical intermediate stage or measured frequency distribution is introduced.

## Phase IV — Temporal event nominals

Temporal `z/do/na/u` are now explicitly licensed with complete indirect event nominals, retaining the established `pjaštou/pjaštově` paradigm, nominal dependents, argument-role ambiguity, anchoring, and postpositional prosody. The new constructions are adopted reform specifications, not claims of prior usage. Source `z` defaults to completion (onset requires contextual identification), with matrix extent distinguishing after/since; endpoint `do` defaults to onset and requires an extended matrix interval or suitable coercion. The endpoint may be projected; matrix non-occurrence before it neither asserts the reference event nor a positive matrix occurrence thereafter. `na` requires overlap, while `u` permits contextual proximity without overlap. `-esny` is retained for potentially unrealised prospective reference; immediate succession requires a context or independent expression fixing immediacy.

The five specialised temporal endings are now withdrawn from live descriptions: `-ezak →` temporal `do`; `-eškady → -ení/na/u`; `-eeby → z`; `-eebymy → -u/z`; `-ešhoume → -u/-ezmy` with an independent immediacy condition. Their earlier design status implies no archaic, dialectal or historical distribution. The Manifest comparison with withdrawn suffixes is removed. The particle regression retains a synthetic substring test instead of requiring the withdrawn suffix in live grammar. Three exact new nominal-interface units are added to the scope fixture; every pre-existing protected unit remains intact.
